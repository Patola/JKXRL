## r_vulkanAutosprites

**Diagnostic A/B**: Rotates authored static BSP autosprite quads toward the shared stereo camera and pivots autosprite2 strips around their fixed long axis.

Values/units: 0: leave eligible quads in their authored orientation; 1: camera-facing (default). Immediate and not archived.

Caveat: Does not control grass, weather, FX sprites or BSP flare records. Restricted to affine, four-vertex static-world quads without mixed wave/bulge/move deformation. Unsupported surfaces are logged. Collision is unchanged.
